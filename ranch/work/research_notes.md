# THE RANCH — EXPANSION RESEARCH NOTES (Al Ain + Abu Dhabi)
Compiled 22 June 2026. Figures carry source + year. Estimates flagged.

## CONSTANTS (from Sharjah deck — do not change)
- Tenure: Musataha / usufruct (40+10yr). Income clears hurdle, no terminal value.
- Macro: community retail under-built across UAE; structural drivers (D33, population, visa) compound.
- Return target: ~13-15% stabilised cash on cash, unlevered IRR 12-14%.
- Look & feel: industrial-village typology, steel portal frames, brick/tile, gabion stone, dark cladding, daylit halls, operable glazing, open-air. British-styled industrial premium.
- LEVERS to adjust per city: Location, Catchment, Whitespace -> Concept (tenant mix). Plus steel:concrete ratio.

## AL AIN  (mandate: fine-dining F&B + world-class gym; targeted verification)
================================================================
DEMOGRAPHICS / WEALTH
- Al Ain region pop ~986,910 (SCAD 2024), +4.4% YoY; ~24% of Abu Dhabi emirate. 2023 census 1,009,735. [High]
- ~30.8% UAE Nationals — highest Emirati concentration of any major UAE city (~3x national norm). "Garden City" heartland. [Med]
- Family, villa-dominant, owner-occupier market; low speculative volatility. [High]
- Avg villa asking ~AED 4.88m (range 1.2-8.0m); broad residential avg ~AED 2.55m (Bayut 2025, asking). [Med]
- Affluent Emirati villa districts: Al Towayya, Al Jimi, Falaj Hazzaa, Asharej, Al Khabisi, Al Muwaiji, Al Maqam, Neima. High-end: Shiab Al Ashkhar (~AED 8.0m), Al Markhaniyyah (~AED 4.25m). [Med]
- Emirate median age 33; 54% aged 25-44 (SCAD 2023/24). Emirati households ~6-8 persons [estimate].

FINE DINING — VERIFIED ACUTE WHITESPACE
- Al Ain = 0 MICHELIN venues; Abu Dhabi city 56; Dubai 100+ (MICHELIN 2026). [High]
- Effectively ZERO standalone contemporary fine-dining venues. Entire upper tier = ~4-5 aging hotel restaurants (Al Ain Rotana: Trader Vic's/Min Zaman/Zest; Danat Al Ain; Radisson Blu; Mercure Jebel Hafeet). Mostly buffets/single-cuisine. [High gap]
- Affluent residents drive to Abu Dhabi/Dubai for higher-end dining = demand leakage. [High]
- CONSTRAINT: alcohol-licensed dining is hotel-only in Al Ain. Model via licensed-hotel partnership or premium destination non-licensed concept.

GYM / WELLNESS
- ONE premium large-format incumbent: Gold's Gym Signature Club (~62,000 sqft; track, pools, MMA). [High]
- GymNation (~40,000 sqft) = value/"most affordable". Rest = value/community/ladies-only/hotel gyms. No Warehouse/F45/Barry's/boutique cluster. [Med]
- => "world-class gym" mandate REAL but partially occupied. Open lane = INTEGRATED premium wellness/longevity/recovery (training + spa + recovery + longevity) — no incumbent. Differentiate here. [High]

RETAIL CONTEXT
- Enclosed-mall dominated: ~7 malls, ~412,000 sqm GLA (Bawadi ~110k, Al Ain Mall ~93k, Al Jimi ~91k, Remal ~47k, Al Foah ~41k, Hili ~30k). Repetitive Carrefour/LuLu/Centrepoint anchors. [Med-High]
- NO operating open-air lifestyle/village retail. [High] Watch-item: Ajwaa Mall (under construction, hybrid open-air, retractable glass).
- Retail per resident projected only ~1.115 sqm by 2030 (Plan Al Ain 2030 / Aldar 2016) — deliberately under-built. [Med]
- Emirate retail 94% occupied, thin pipeline (CBRE/JLL 2025).

TOURISM / DESTINATION / PIPELINE
- 473,100 hotel guests FY2025 (+9%), ~66% occupancy, leisure-led (Abu Dhabi Media Office/DCT 2025). DCT 2030 target ~520,000 overnight leisure guests. [High]
- DCT pillars: culture + wellness + adventure. Jebel Hafeet (~1,249m, Green Mubazzarah hot springs), Al Ain Oasis (UNESCO 2011), Al Ain Zoo, Wadi Adventure, Al Jahili Fort. Gulf Tourism Capital 2025; Arab Capital of Tourism 2026. [High]
- Pipeline: 10,316 villas across 5 developments within AED 106bn AD housing plan (Aldar/Bloom/Lead/IMKAN) (Arabian Post 2026). Aldar Al Ain: Al Jimi Mall, Remal Mall, Al Oyoun Village (148 villas/TH), Shabhat Plaza (1,045 national units). Ain Al Fayda masterplan (Stantec). [High totals]
- CORRECTION: Alreeman/Fay Alreeman/Reeman Living are in Al Shamkha (AD city), NOT Al Ain.

AL AIN WHITESPACE VERDICT
- PRIMARY: Fine dining (acute, verified). Integrated premium wellness/longevity (no incumbent). Open-air lifestyle retail (near-absent).
- Gym = qualify mandate: large-format faces Gold's; win via integrated wellness/recovery/longevity + boutique studios.
- Complementary: specialty/gourmet grocer (vs mass hypermarkets), boutique fitness, structured recovery, children's enrichment, destination/experiential leisure aligned to DCT wellness-adventure + tourism.

## ABU DHABI  (open-book whitespace; NOT a copy of F&B+supermarket+gym)
================================================================
DEMOGRAPHICS / GROWTH
- AD emirate pop 4.14m (SCAD 2024), +7.5% YoY, +51% over the decade. Median age 33. Emirati TFR 3.1 vs UAE ~1.46 (World Bank 2023). [High]
- 26.6m visitors 2025 -> target 39.3m by 2030 (DCT 2026). [High]
- Physical-activity participation 60.3% in 2025 (from 53.6% in 2024) (Abu Dhabi Sports Council 2025). [High]
- ~660k children; 56.5% of UAE households couples-with-children (Euromonitor 2024). 39,333 births in AD 2024 (+4.5%) (DoH/SCAD 2025). [High]

RESIDENTIAL GROWTH CORRIDORS
- Residential stock ~315,000 units (Cavendish Maxwell Q3 2025); pipeline ~12,800 (2026), ~12,400 (2027), ~21,400 (2028). Apartments +14.8% y/y, villas +11.8% (2025). [High]
- Aldar FY2025 sales AED 40.6bn (+21%); handing over 3,500+ units in 2026; UAE backlog AED 61bn. [High]
- Communities: Saadiyat Lagoons (villas, handover 2026); Fahid Island (GDV AED 40bn, 6,000+ homes); Yas Acres/Yas Park Gate (508 homes); Hudayriyat Island (Modon, >51M sqm); Al Reem; Alreeman/Fay Alreeman (Al Shamkha: Fay I 554 villas, Fay II 557, Reeman Living 1,050 apts; Alreeman I ~22,000 residents target). [Med-High]
- UNDER-SERVED corridor: Al Shamkha – Al Mizn – Madinat Al Riyadh (mainland southern villa belt). Retail delivery LAGS residential by 3-5 years. Residents drive 15-25 min (Mussafah/Yas/Khalifa City) for daily needs. [Med]

RETAIL SUPPLY
- Total AD retail GLA ~3.75m sqm, 94% occupancy (ADREC 2025); supply growing only ~2.3%/yr; rents +8% YoY. [High]
- Per capita ~0.91 sqm vs Dubai ~1.24 [estimate; under-supplied vs Dubai but conflicting Statista — directional].
- Community + street retail ~44% of GLA. Strongest demand theme: convenience/community retail in residential communities (JLL 2025). Avoid undifferentiated enclosed super-regional mall GLA. [High]

WHITESPACE CATEGORIES (the open-book core; ranked)
1. LONGEVITY / PREVENTATIVE MEDICAL-WELLNESS — PRIMARY. DoH created world-first "Healthy Longevity Medicine Centre" license (Oct 2024) + Declaration on Longevity & Precision Medicine (ADGHW 2025). M42: largest Omics CoE outside US; Emirati Genome Program >800,000 genomes (target 1m); biobank >900,000 samples; Juvenescence partnership. CCAD >10,000 intl patients 2024 (+35%). Only ~2 HLMC-licensed facilities (IHLAD, PureHealth Pura). Dedicated longevity cluster sits in Dubai, not AD. [High demand / Med supply gap]
2. BOUTIQUE FITNESS / REFORMER PILATES / RECOVERY (AD) — PRIMARY. Marquee brands only recently entering AD (Warehouse, Barry's ~1, Crank ~5); reformer/Lagree clustering Reem/Saadiyat/Al Maryah, NO category leader = land-grab. Riding +6.7pt/yr participation. Athlete recovery thin (reSTART "first and only"). [Med-High]
3. AUTISM / SPECIAL-NEEDS / ABA THERAPY — PRIMARY (clearest single gap). ZHO 19 centres serve only 522 autism students vs ~4,500-6,600 estimated children. DoH first ABA Guidelines Sept 2024. Demand structurally outpaces supply. [High demand / Med supply]
4. NEIGHBOURHOOD STEAM / EDUTAINMENT — PRIMARY-to-SECONDARY. ~660k children; 56.5% households w/ children; FEC market >US$430m (2024-30); year-round indoor need. KidZania (Yas) essentially only branded anchor (role-play, not science/maker). Under-served middle = destination hands-on STEAM/discovery. Risk: fastest-filling. [High directional]
5. INDOOR RACQUET / PADEL / PICKLEBALL (boutique, AC) — SECONDARY (window closing). AD ~50+ padel courts; UAE 950+ courts/450k players; saturating, only indoor/AC/boutique viable. Pickleball early (~16 courts AD). Best as multi-sport amenity. [Med]
SECONDARY/AVOID: Paediatric sub-specialty SECONDARY (only 3 paed rheumatologists emirate-wide); Mental health SECONDARY (Sakina built aggressively, 40+ facilities); Women's health core IVF SATURATED (14 fertility centres, 6,180+ cycles 2024); Large attractions AVOID (Miral/Yas, Disneyland AD announced May 2025).

STRATEGIC POSITIONING (govt direction to align with)
- DoH longevity/precision-medicine state bet; Abu Dhabi Sports Council activity push (60.3%); DCT family-tourism (38% of top Indian source market travel with children); culture (Saadiyat: Louvre, Guggenheim, Zayed National Museum, teamLab).

ABU DHABI CONCEPT THESIS
- NOT supermarket+gym+generic F&B. Reframe The Ranch as a HEALTH, LONGEVITY & FAMILY-PERFORMANCE village.
- Anchors/footfall: (1) longevity & preventative-wellness clinic; (2) boutique fitness + recovery cluster; (3) specialist family health (autism/ABA + paediatric sub-specialty); (4) STEAM children's discovery. Base-load support: curated healthy F&B + specialty food hall/grocer; indoor racquet amenity.
- Location/catchment lever: target under-served mainland villa growth corridor (Al Shamkha / Zayed City / Alreeman belt) where retail lags residential 3-5 yrs and Emirati-weighted family villa catchments are forming.

## MATERIAL (steel:concrete) RATIO RATIONALE (both Musataha, steel-led)
- Al Ain (fine-dining premium, destination, oasis/mountain context): tilt toward warmth/permanence -> ~55:45 steel:concrete (gabion stone, brick, more masonry).
- Abu Dhabi (longevity/clinical/performance, daylit, clean): tilt lighter/cleaner -> ~70:30 steel:concrete (more glazing, lightweight, daylit, clinical-grade).
